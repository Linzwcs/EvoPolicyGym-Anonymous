Crafter

This ecosystem contains the independently installable EvoPolicyGym Benchmark distribution powered by Crafter.

  • crafter/ publishes two reward profiles over the pinned Crafter 1.8.3 simulator: the default long-horizon lhs objective and the native canonical achievement objective.
  • Both profiles support the original 64 x 64 x 3 RGB observation and the explicitly selected, separately identified local-symbolic-v1 observation.
  • Training Feedback can contain complete compressed trajectories and lossless Policy observations. Validation and held-out test Feedback remains aggregate-only.

The distribution is not included in the base EvoPolicyGym wheel. See crafter/README.md for its complete contract and local launcher.
