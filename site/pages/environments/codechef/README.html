CodeChef environments

CodeChef challenge problems are integrated only through independently written simulators based on their public problem contracts. No CodeChef statements, submissions, generators, test data, or other contest assets are redistributed.

Suites are grouped by their original contest. Each runnable leaf remains an independently installable Benchmark distribution.
